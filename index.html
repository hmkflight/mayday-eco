Why we do this

Small acts of kindness can mean more than we realize. A wish granted, a moment of generosity, or simply choosing to show up for someone can remind a person that they are seen, valued, and cared for. We believe those moments are worth sharing.

mayday.eco exists to make that kindness visible. By sharing the stories behind wishes granted through One Simple Wish, we hope to create a ripple effect — one person sees someone else give, feels inspired, and decides to give too. Because sometimes, seeing kindness is all it takes to pass it on.

kindness is contagious — that's the whole idea
